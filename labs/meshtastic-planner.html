Tailored for Ann Arbor & Washtenaw County terrain · Optimized for low channel utilization (<12% airtime) & Michigan winter idle survival

Why Roof Base = CLIENT (Not ROUTER)

At ~875 ft ASL (Downtown Ann Arbor ~840 ft ground + 35 ft roof/mast), a central base node covers the Huron valley basin well, while 960–985 ft moraine crests (Barton Hills & West Side) command horizon sightlines. Setting it to CLIENT lets it smartly repeat packets after listening briefly, whereas ROUTER preemptively grabs hops and can swallow packets meant for higher hilltop relays.

Zero-Collision Idle Intervals

Default Meshtastic settings broadcast GPS & telemetry too frequently, waking radios and draining batteries. For a quiet, reliable Idle Mesh, set stationary nodes to fixed GPS coordinates, node_info_broadcast_secs = 10800 (3 hrs), and device_update_interval = 3600 (1 hr).

Michigan Winter Solar Math

Southeast Michigan averages only 1.45 Peak Sun Hours/day in December. An nRF52840 (RAK4631) draws just ~9.5 mA idle (~0.23 Ah/day = 13+ days dark runtime on one 3000mAh 18650), whereas an ESP32 (Heltec V3) draws ~95 mA (~2.28 Ah/day = dies in 31 hrs without deep sleep).
