Ideal / Idle Mesh Architecture, Solar Autonomy & CLI Generator

Tailored for Southeast Michigan / Northville terrain · Optimized for low channel utilization (<12% airtime) & Michigan winter idle survival

Why Chimney = CLIENT (Not ROUTER)

At 886 ft ASL (853 ft base + 33 ft chimney), your home node has great reach East toward Livonia, but the 960+ ft moraine ridges to the West block horizon sightlines. Setting it to CLIENT lets it smartly repeat packets after listening briefly, whereas ROUTER preemptively grabs hops and can swallow packets meant for higher hilltop nodes (like Maybury State Park at 971 ft).

Zero-Collision Idle Intervals

Default Meshtastic settings broadcast GPS & telemetry too frequently, waking radios and draining batteries. For a quiet, reliable Idle Mesh, set stationary nodes to fixed GPS coordinates, node_info_broadcast_secs = 10800 (3 hrs), and device_update_interval = 3600 (1 hr).

Michigan Winter Solar Math

Southeast Michigan averages only 1.45 Peak Sun Hours/day in December. An nRF52840 (RAK4631) draws just ~9.5 mA idle (~0.23 Ah/day = 13+ days dark runtime on one 3000mAh 18650), whereas an ESP32 (Heltec V3) draws ~95 mA (~2.28 Ah/day = dies in 31 hrs without deep sleep).
