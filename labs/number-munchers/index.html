THE 4 MECC MUNCHERS GAMES (1985-1991)

  • Word Munchers (1985): Phonics (short, long, and team vowel sounds), Parts of Speech, and Synonyms/Antonyms.
  • Number Munchers (1986/1990): Multiples, Factors, Primes, Equality, and Inequality.
  • Fraction Munchers (1987): Proper/Improper/Reduced Fractions, Equivalents, Comparing, and Fraction Expressions.
  • Super Munchers (1991): Trivia categories across Animals, Space/Geography, Science, and Culture. Eat 10 right in a row to become Super Muncher and gobble Troggles!

THE 5 TROGGLES

    CONTROLS & SCORING

    • Arrow Keys / WASD: Move Muncher one square
    • Spacebar / Enter: Munch the current square
    • Click / Tap: Auto-walk to any square (click current square to Munch)
    • ESC / F1 / F2 / F3 / F4 / P / M / F: Menu / Info / Options / Hall of Fame / Switch Disk / Pause / Sound / Fullscreen
    • Classic Scoring: 5 × Level pts per match, 50 × Level clear bonus, free Muncher every 1,000 pts!
